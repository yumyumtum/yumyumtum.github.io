THE PLAN

One main focus each day

No hotel moves, limited walking, and plenty of time to pause. Each map opens the complete route in Google Maps.

01

ARRIVAL DAY · SLEEP IN PORT ANGELES

Sammamish → Edmonds → Kingston → Port Angeles

Drive from Sammamish to the Edmonds terminal. Take your car onto the ferry for the 30-minute crossing, then continue to Port Angeles. After check-in, plan only dinner and rest.

Allow 4–5 hours, including the ferry queue and sailing

02

COAST DAY · RETURN TO THE SAME HOTEL

Lake Crescent → Ruby Beach → Kalaloch → Port Angeles

Make a short lakeside stop at Lake Crescent, then focus the afternoon on the coast. Ruby Beach has sea stacks; Kalaloch is a broad sandy beach for sitting, strolling, and sunset. If anyone is tired, visit Ruby Beach only and return to the hotel.

About 4 hours round trip for Ruby; about 5 hours with Kalaloch

03

MOUNTAIN VIEWS & RETURN

Hurricane Ridge → Kingston → Edmonds → Sammamish

Drive up to Hurricane Ridge for mountain views without a long hike. Then return to Kingston, ferry back to Edmonds, and drive home to Sammamish.

Allow 4–5 hours, including the ferry and breaks

FERRY HOW-TO

Taking the ferry with your car

  1. Drive to the Edmonds terminal; on a summer weekday, arrive 45–60 minutes early.
  2. Wait in the vehicle queue. You can stretch nearby, but stay close enough to move your car.
  3. Follow crew directions onto the ferry, turn off and lock the car, then use the passenger cabin or open deck.
  4. Return to the car before arrival and drive off at Kingston.

HIGHLIGHTS

What you will see

Hurricane Ridge

Big mountain views by car; the visitor center and restrooms make it a comfortable stop for grandparents.

Lake Crescent

Stop for a lake view and lunch—no need to commit to the longer waterfall walk.

Ruby + Kalaloch

Ruby has sea stacks and tide pools; Kalaloch is a broad beach for a gentle stroll and sunset.

A realistic plan for two grandparents with limited mobility

This itinerary treats hiking as optional, not required. Plan one main outing each day and keep the same Port Angeles hotel for both nights. Confirm an accessible or ground-floor room, walk-in shower, elevator, parking, and the distance to breakfast or the lobby before booking.
